## Selected Research and Projects

### HPAI-H5N1 Dairy Epidemiological Modelling

[HPAI-H5N1-Dairy-Epidemiological-Modelling](https://github.com/ekisa-shadrack-philemon/HPAI-H5N1-Dairy-Epidemiological-Modelling)

**Role:** Lead Analyst and Co-author

A longitudinal investigation of the temporal relationship between HPAI H5N1 2024 outbreak and changes in dairy cattle milk production, cell count and rumination patterns in a commercial dairy herd in Minnesota, USA.

The analytical work incorporates generalized additive mixed models (GAMMs), interrupted time-series analysis, autoregressive error structures, change-point analysis, and cow-level behavioural monitoring.

**Related Manuscript:** *The Temporal Relationship Between Highly Pathogenic Avian Influenza (HPAI H5N1) Outbreak and Changes in Milk Production* — currently under peer review.
